# PRIVACY POLICY FOR PAROT

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

IT Amadersomaj Inc ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the Parot desktop application ("Parot", "Application") available on the Microsoft Store.

1. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT
Parot is an entirely offline desktop companion. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal information, personally identifiable data (PII), device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, or usage telemetry to any external servers, third parties, or cloud services.

2. LOCAL DATA STORAGE
All application settings, customization choices (such as bird skin, accessories, volume, and language preferences), and wellness statistics (such as daily active minutes and completed Pomodoro sessions) are stored strictly locally on your computer in your local Application Data directory (%AppData%\Parot). You can reset or delete this data at any time by clearing your local app data or uninstalling the application.

3. MICROPHONE & AUDIO PROCESSING
Parot features optional voice recognition and audio echo mimicry. When you speak voice commands or record a voice mimic sample:
- Audio signals are processed entirely in real-time on your local machine using standard Windows Speech APIs.
- Audio recordings are processed locally and are NEVER transmitted, uploaded, streamed, or shared over the internet or to external cloud servers.
- Microphone access is completely optional; all core pet animations, window perching, and mini-games function without microphone permissions.

4. NETWORK ACCESS
Parot does not require an active internet connection to function. If live weather display is enabled, the app only performs an anonymous public meteorological query without transmitting personal identifiers.

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & ADVERTISING
Parot contains no advertisements, no analytics trackers, no third-party marketing SDKs, and no cookies.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Because Parot does not collect any personal data, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations. Parot is completely safe for users of all ages.

7. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected in future releases of the application.
