### ✨ Key Highlights & Features
- **100% Autonomous Avian Life Cycle:**
  - Audible dual-frequency claw tap footsteps (`620Hz` and `530Hz`).
  - Natural parabolic hopping with landing squash and cheerful chirps.
  - Complete body grooming: preening, wing stretches, head scratching, beak wiping, feather fluffing, tail wagging, and yawns.
  - Daytime power naps and automatic nocturnal sleep schedule.
  - Foraging and hunting live insects (Ladybugs 🐞 and Grasshoppers 🦗).
- **Smart Window Perching & Multi-Monitor Roaming:**
  - Perches on active application titlebars and tracks window dragging.
  - DWM cloaked window protection prevents floating in empty air when no tabs are open.
  - Smooth multi-monitor drag-and-drop placement (`GetMonitorWorkAreaFromPoint`).
- **60 FPS Aerodynamic Flight Physics:**
  - Quadratic Bezier curves with dynamic banking angles (-25° to +25°).
- **🏰 Golden Birdcage Mode:**
  - Securely houses Parot in an elegant golden cage for peaceful in-place companionship.
- **🍅 Pomodoro & Wellness Guardian:**
  - 25m Pomodoro focus sprints with 5m dance breaks.
  - 20-20-20 eye strain breaks, hydration prompts, and posture checks.
- **🦜 5 Species & 7 Accessories:**
  - Scarlet Macaw, Green Parakeet, Blue & Gold Macaw, Cockatiel, and White Cockatoo.
  - Crowns, Pirate Hats, Sunglasses, Bowties, Top Hats, and Headphones.
- **🎮 Interactive Mini-Games:**
  - Toss treats (chilis, peanuts, seeds) for Parot to catch mid-air.
  - Bouncy football push, laser pointer chase, and interactive desktop mirror.
- **🎙️ Voice Recognition & Pitch Mimicry:**
  - Offline voice commands (`fly`, `dance`, `sleep`, `eat`, `cage`, `come here`).
  - Records and echoes your speech back in hilarious high-pitched parrot vocalizations.
- **🔒 100% Offline & Private:**
  - Zero telemetry, zero tracking, runs strictly on your local PC.
